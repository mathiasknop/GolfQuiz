# GolfQuiz

A React-based golf quiz and scoring app built for **The National Golf Brussels**.

## About

Interactive quiz night application featuring 9 rounds of golf trivia:

- **Series 1–7** — Themed question rounds (Ryder Cup, New Superstars, Women's Golf, Golf in Belgium, Old Superstars, The Open, The Majors)
- **Varia Round** — 15 mixed golf knowledge questions
- **Photo Round** — Identify 10 golf legends from photos

Supports up to 10 teams with live scoring, answer reveals, and a leaderboard.

## Tech Stack

- React (JSX) with hooks
- Single-file component (`WebApp/golf-quiz.jsx`)
- Styled with The National Golf Brussels brand colors
